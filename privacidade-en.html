Privacy policy

Babá do Pet, version 0.43. Updated on 10/30/2026.

Summary

Babá do Pet has no sign-up, no ads, and does not send your pet's data to any server of ours. Everything you log is saved only on your device. The only information that leaves is an anonymous usage measurement (which screens were opened).

Who is responsible

Wanderson, an individual, the app's developer.

Contact: suporte.babapet@gmail.com

What the app stores on your device

  • Your pets' data: name, species, breed, sex, birth date and profile photo.
  • Vaccines and other health records, weigh-ins, food plan, meals and expenses.
  • Album photos and videos, schedule appointments and the pet's health plan data, if you add them.
  • If you create the optional login: the e-mail you entered and the password as an irreversible code (hash), plus a biometrics flag. This stays only on the device and is never sent to anyone.
  • Copies of the app itself, so it opens even without internet.

This data lives in your device's browser storage. We have no access to it and cannot recover it if you delete it.

What leaves your device

Some features talk to third-party services. This happens only in the cases below:

  • Fonts: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives the internet address (IP) and technical information about your browser, as with any site that uses this service.
  • Hosting: the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the internet address of visitors to the site, according to its own policy.
  • Searching other animals: the name you type is sent to Wikipedia to find the text and image. Nothing else about your pet is sent.
  • Partners: when a partner is listed and you tap their button, the app opens WhatsApp with a ready message. It may include your pet's name, the vaccine name or the food product. The message is only sent if you confirm in WhatsApp.
  • Pet events: the event list is a file on the site itself (eventos.json). The app does not send your location or the state filter you choose; it stays only on your device.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you tap "Sign in with Google" in More, the app loads Google's sign-in code and asks for access only to the app's hidden folder in your Google Drive, plus your name and e-mail to show who is connected. The full backup (data, photos and videos) goes straight from your device to your Drive and does not pass through any server of ours; only you and the app can access that folder. You can delete the backup in the app or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Google's use follows Google's policy. If you don't use this feature, nothing is sent to Google because of it.
  • Anonymous usage measurement: the app may send to our database (Supabase) only event names, such as "opened the app", "opened the Schedule screen", "viewed Babá's introduction", "sent alarms to the calendar" (only the count), "created an alarm" (only the type, such as food or training, with no text or time), "an alarm fired", "played the puzzle" (only the level and the number of moves; photos never leave the device), "added a pet" (with the species, for example dog or cat) or "installed the app", the type and size of the file when a photo can't be read (never the photo), plus a random number generated on the device, the time zone, the site the link came from and whether the app is installed. We do not send the pet's name, photos, amounts, health dates, e-mail or location. It serves to learn where people get stuck and to improve the app. If you want the records tied to your device deleted, ask through the contact above saying you used the app.
  • Sources and external links: when you tap a link, you leave the app and the policy of the opened site applies.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't receive it.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or third-party trackers (such as Google Analytics). The usage measurement is our own and anonymous.
  • We don't ask for your name, e-mail, phone or location.

If the app ever gets accounts or starts collecting other data, this policy will be updated first and you will be notified inside the app.

Your rights

Since the data is with you, you control it:

  • Take it with you: in More, tap Export backup. The "Full backup" also carries the album photos and videos, in a .zip file; the "Data only" is light and does not include the album.
  • Delete a pet: tap its name to edit and choose Delete. This also deletes its records and album.
  • Delete everything: in More, tap Delete all data. Clearing the site data in the browser also removes everything.

For other questions about your personal data, contact us using the address above.

Children

The app is aimed at adult pet owners and does not collect data from anyone.

Health notice

The Library content is informational and under veterinary review. It does not replace a visit to a veterinarian. In an emergency, see a veterinarian.
