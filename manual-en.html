1. What Babá do Pet is

It's your digital pet sitter. It helps you remember vaccines, food, weight, expenses, walks, baths, medication and training, with alarms and even a puzzle to play with. It works for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, rodents, reptiles and other animals.

Babá, the app's meerkat, appears on the Home screen and changes expression depending on your pet's situation. The first 5 times you open the app, she introduces herself and shows what it can do. To see it again any time, go to More › Meet the app.

ImportantThe app organizes and reminds, but it doesn't replace the veterinarian. Vaccines, medication and health questions should be decided with them.

2. Install on your phone

The app opens in the browser and can sit as an icon on your phone screen, like any other app.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the app's address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top corner.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

If the browser offers installation, the Install the app button also appears in the More tab.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the app's address in Safari.
  2. Tap the share button (a square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
TipIn More there is a Check installation button. It checks that the app's icon is set up correctly.

Once installed, the app opens even without internet. Updates arrive on their own when you're connected.

3. Add your pet

  1. The first time, tap Add my pet. After that, use + Pet at the top of the screen.
  2. Fill in the name, species, breed or type, sex and birth date.
  3. If you like, choose a photo. It appears in a round frame before you save.
  4. Tap Add.
TipFill in the breed or type (for example, chinchilla, cockatiel or tortoise). The Library guide uses this information.

More than one pet: each pet appears as a button at the top. Tap the name to switch. The screens always show the data of the selected pet.

Edit or delete: tap the already-selected pet (the highlighted one) at the top again to open editing. With Delete, the app asks for confirmation, and also deletes its vaccines, weight, food, expenses, schedule and health plan.

4. Home screen

  • Babá gives a message of the day, such as a vaccine coming up or food running out.
  • Four tiles show the summary of Health, Food, Weight and the month's Expenses. Tap a tile to open its screen.
  • Days caring: counts how many days in a row you've logged something.
  • Schedule: shows how many appointments are for today or overdue.
  • Shortcuts to the Library, Album, Alarms and Puzzle.
  • Log now: quick buttons to feed, note the weight and log an expense.

5. Health

Vaccines and other records

  1. Open the Health tab and tap Add.
  2. Choose the type (vaccine, dewormer, flea & tick, vet visit, exam, medication or other) and the name.
  3. Enter the date it was done and the date of the next one.

Each item shows a label: Up to date, In 5d (5 days left), Today or 3d overdue (3 days late). To fix something, tap the item.

For dogs and cats, the app works with routine vaccines and dewormers. For other animals, it shows vet visits, exams and treatments, because their vaccine schedule is set by the veterinarian.

Heads upThe "Done on" date can't be in the future. To schedule something, use "Next date".

Pet health plan

  1. At the top of the Health tab, tap Add health plan.
  2. Enter the provider and, if you like, the plan name, member card, monthly fee, due day, waiting period, renewal and phone.
  3. Use Log a claim for each vet visit or exam: the total cost and how much the plan paid.

The app shows how much the plan covered in the year and how much you paid. It also alerts you in the Schedule when the monthly fee, the renewal or the end of the waiting period is coming up. The Call the plan button appears when you enter the phone number.

6. Schedule

Use the Schedule tab for walks, training, baths, grooming, vet visits, medication, contests and events.

Create an appointment

  1. Tap a type at the top (for example, Walk) or New appointment.
  2. Choose the date and, if you like, the time, place and notes.
  3. Under Repeat, choose every day, every week, every 15 days, every month or every year.
  4. For contests and events, also fill in Register by.

Mark as done

Tap the ✔ Done button. If the appointment repeats, the app sets the next date right away. If it doesn't repeat, it moves to "Recently done".

Appointments are grouped: Overdue, Today, Next 7 days and Later on. Vaccines due within 30 days also appear in the Schedule.

Get alerts on your phone

The app doesn't send notifications on its own. To be alerted, tap Add to phone calendar. The app downloads a file; open it and your phone's calendar adds everything, with repeats and a reminder 30 minutes before each appointment. Do it again whenever you create new appointments.

Pet events

Below the appointments there is a list of shows, contests, adoption fairs and meetups. By default it shows the selected pet's events; tap All pets to see all of them, and use the state filter. Save to schedule copies the event to the pet's schedule.

Heads upThe list is built and reviewed by hand. Confirm the date, place and prices on the event's official site before going.

7. Food

  1. The first time, tap Set up food. Enter the product, brand, total per day (in grams), meals per day, the bag size and price and when it was opened.
  2. The app works out how many days are left until the food runs out.
  3. Each time you feed, tap Feed. The meal is logged under "Latest meals".
  4. When you open a new bag, tap I opened a new bag.

To change the portion or the bag, use Edit plan. To delete a meal logged by mistake, tap the trash icon next to it.

8. Weight

The Weight tab is under More › Weight, and also in the Weight tile on the Home screen.

  1. Tap Log weight and enter the date and the weight in kilograms.
  2. From two weigh-ins on, the app shows a chart of the progress.
  3. The history is right below; you can delete a wrong weigh-in.

9. Expenses

  1. Tap New expense, choose the category (food, vet visit, vaccine, grooming, training and others), the amount and the date.
  2. The app shows the month's total and how much went to each category.
  3. Use the month arrows to see previous months.
  4. To fix or delete, tap the entry.

10. Photo and video albums

Open it from the shortcut on the Home screen or in More › Album.

  • Two albums: "Cute moments" and "Contests and events". Switch with the buttons at the top.
  • Photos and videos: use the tabs to switch and Add photos or Add videos to include some. Videos can be up to 80 MB.
  • Three ways to view: Grid, Frames (like developed photos) and Carousel (swipe sideways). The app remembers your choice.
  • When you open a photo or video: write a caption, mark it as a favorite, share, download or delete. Swipe sideways to go to the next one.
ImportantPhotos and videos stay only on your device. To avoid losing them, make the full backup (see section 14).

11. Species library

Open it from the shortcut on Home or in More. It offers a care guide, allowed foods and dangerous foods for several species.

  • The guide covers almost 80 animals and breeds: dogs (German Shepherd, Doberman, Rottweiler, Shih Tzu, Golden, Labrador, Poodle and others), cats, birds, rodents, reptiles and freshwater fish (betta, pleco, zebrafish, angelfish, corydoras and others) and saltwater fish (clownfish, Dory, tangs and others).
  • Tap the group (Dogs, Cats, Birds…) and pick the animal or breed from the list. If the pet's profile has the breed, the app opens the right guide on its own.
  • Use the search by name to find the animal. If it isn't on the list, the app searches Wikipedia.
  • Use the food checker to find out if something is harmful to your pet.
Heads upThe guides are informational and still under veterinary review. If in doubt, see a veterinarian, especially for wild and exotic animals.

12. Alarms

Alarms for food, medication, training, walks and anything else you need to remember. Open from the Home screen or in More › Alarms. Each pet has its own.

Create an alarm

  1. Tap New alarm.
  2. Choose the type (food, medication, training, walk, water or other) and, if you like, write a description, such as "Morning meal".
  3. Set the time and the days of the week.
  4. Choose the sound and use Hear the sound to test it. There are beeps and signals (simple beep, digital alert, bell, alarm clock, siren, truck beep), animal sounds (little bird, meow, bark, rooster, horse, little frog and others) and even applause.
  5. Choose the alert window: Babá (the meerkat), Surprise critter (a different animal each time) or Simple (just the icon).
  6. Tap Save.

Tap an alarm in the list to edit or delete it. The On/Off button turns it on and off without deleting.

When the alarm rings

At the set time, the app plays the sound repeatedly for up to 1 minute and opens the alert window with three buttons: Done, Snooze 10 min (alerts again in 10 minutes) and Dismiss. This happens while the app is open or in the background. Use Test the first alarm to see how it looks. If the Allow on-screen alerts on the device button appears, tap it so the app also shows an alert when it's in the background.

Alarm with the app closed: use your phone's calendar

The browser doesn't let an app ring by itself when it's closed. For your phone to alert you anyway, send the alarms to the calendar:

  1. On the Alarms screen, tap Send to calendar.
  2. On your phone, choose Calendar (iPhone) or Google Calendar (Android) on the screen that opens. If a file downloads, open it.
  3. Tap Add.

Done: the calendar alerts you on time, with the app closed and without internet, using the phone's own sound. The animal sounds and the cute window only appear with the app open.

Heads upThe calendar doesn't follow what you change in the app. If you edit or delete an alarm, send it to the calendar again and delete the old event there.

13. Puzzle

A game to have fun with your pet. Open it from the Home screen (card with the New! badge) or in More › Puzzle.

  1. Choose the level: Easy 4×4 (16 pieces), Medium 6×6 (36) or Hard 9×9 (81).
  2. The picture is a surprise: it can be a photo from your pet's album or an exotic critter. The more photos in the album, the more photos of your pet appear.
  3. Tap two pieces to swap them. Pieces already in the right place get a green border.
  4. If you need help, tap See image. Shuffle mixes the same picture again.

The app counts moves and time and keeps your best result for each level, only on this device. When you finish, Play again picks another picture.

14. Your data: backup and switching phones

The app has no sign-up and no cloud. Everything stays on your device. So make a backup now and then.

Heads upAlarms and puzzle records stay only on this device and are not yet included in the backup. If you switch phones, create the alarms again.

Make the backup

In More › Your data:

  • Full backup (with album): creates a .zip file with everything, including photos and videos. It can be large.
  • Data only (light): creates a .json file without the photos and videos.

The file goes to the downloads folder. Keep a copy in your e-mail, Drive or computer.

Restore or switch phones

  1. Install the app on the new phone.
  2. Transfer the backup file to it.
  3. In More, tap Import backup and choose the file.
  4. Confirm. The app shows how many pets, records, photos and videos it will bring.
Heads upImporting replaces the device's data. The app keeps a safety copy of what was there before.

Google Drive backup (when available)

If the Google Drive backup card appears in More, tap Sign in with Google, accept the permission and use Back up now. The full backup goes to a hidden folder in your own Drive. On the new phone, sign in with the same account and tap Restore from Drive. The backup is manual: do it again whenever you want to keep the latest changes.

The More menu

When you tap More, a vertical list opens, with an icon for each option: Species library, Album, Alarms, Puzzle, Weight, Login and lock, Backup and data, User manual, Contact us and Privacy. Tap an option to go straight to it. All options opens the full More screen. To close the menu, tap outside it or Close.

Other items on the More screen

  • Language: switch between Português and English. By default the app follows your phone's language.
  • Meet the app: Babá shows again what you can do.
  • App update: the Check for updates button checks right away for a new version and updates without erasing your data.
  • Contact us: opens your e-mail to write to support (suporte.babapet@gmail.com) with questions, suggestions or problems.
  • Privacy: opens the privacy policy.
  • Demo: opens the app with a sample pet (Thor), without touching your data.
  • Delete all data: erases everything on this device. It can't be undone.

15. Login and lock (optional)

If more people use your phone, you can protect the app. On the Home screen, Babá mentions this in a card with the Go to More button (tap Not now to dismiss). Or go to More → Login and lock → Create login, enter an e-mail and a password of at least 6 characters.

  1. Write down the recovery code that appears after creating the login. It only appears once and is the only way to change the password if you forget it.
  2. With Turn on biometrics, you sign in with your phone's fingerprint or face, if it has one.
  3. The app asks for the password when opened and after about 2 minutes off screen. With Lock now you lock it immediately.
  4. After 5 wrong passwords, the app makes you wait a few seconds.

Important: it's a lock on this device only. It isn't a cloud account, the e-mail doesn't receive messages and the password can't be recovered by e-mail. Saved data isn't encrypted. If you lose both the password and the code, the only way out is to erase all data and start over (or restore from a backup).

16. Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet?

Not for everyday use. Only the search for other animals, the updated event list and external links need internet.

Does my data go anywhere?

Your pet's data (name, photos, vaccines, amounts) stays only on your device. The app sends just an anonymous usage measurement, such as which screens were opened, with no pet data. See the details in the privacy policy at privacidade-en.html.

I lost my data. Now what?

If you have a backup file, use Import backup. Without a backup, it can't be recovered, because there is no cloud copy. Clearing browser data, uninstalling the app or switching phones erases everything.

The old icon or name is still on my phone screen.

If you installed before the name change, remove the shortcut and install again from the browser. Your data stays saved if you don't clear the browser data, but make a backup first, to be safe.

How do I update the app?

It updates itself when it opens with internet, without erasing anything. To check right away, go to More › Check for updates. The current version appears at the bottom of the More screen.

The reminders don't ring on their own.

That's expected: with the app closed, the browser doesn't let it ring. Use Send to calendar on the Alarms screen (or Add to phone calendar in the Schedule) to get alerts from the calendar itself, even with the app closed.

I opened the app and it's empty, without my pet.

Data is kept separate by web address. If you added the pet while opening one address and now opened another (for example, the old one and babadopet.com.br), the app appears empty. Open it again from the same address or icon you used before. To bring the data to the new address, make a full backup on the old one and use Import backup on the new one.

Is the puzzle picture always a surprise?

Yes. The app picks between your pet's album photos and the exotic critters, and avoids repeating the previous game's picture. If there are no photos in the album, only the critters come up.

The photo or video won't go into the album.

Videos are over 80 MB or the phone is out of space. Free up space or choose a smaller video. The album needs a current browser; in very old browsers it doesn't open.

My animal is exotic and isn't on the list.

Add it with the closest species, or "Other", and write the type in the breed field. The app accepts any animal. The Library can search for the animal by name.

I forgot my login password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter the recovery code you wrote down when you created the login. Without the code, the only way out is No code: erase everything and start over. So write down the code and make backups.

How do I contact the developer?

Write to suporte.babapet@gmail.com or use More › Contact us.
